Before you submit — changes, cancellations & what happens next

Submitting this request does not reserve your date and does not create a contract. Griffin will reply within 1–2 business days with availability and a written proposal. Your date is confirmed only after you approve that proposal and sign the event agreement.

Once an event is confirmed and paid, cancellation refunds are: 21+ days before the event — 100%; 8–20 days before — 50%; 7 days or fewer — nonrefundable.
