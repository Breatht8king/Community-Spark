Community partnerships

A smarter way to fund better resident events.

Community Spark connects apartment communities with local businesses that want to support residents in the neighborhoods they serve. The property receives a stronger event at a lower net cost; the sponsor gains meaningful local visibility through a positive experience.

For apartment communities

A credit on your invoice. A better event for residents. Your call.

When a local business sponsors one of your events, their contribution works two ways at once: part of it comes straight off your community's final invoice, and the rest goes into upgrading the event itself — better food, entertainment, décor, prizes, or photography your residents will actually notice. It's not a trade-off between saving money and putting on a great event. Sponsorship gets you both, and Community Spark handles all the outreach and vetting so there's no extra work on your end.

  • A direct credit that lowers the property's final event invoice
  • Real upgrades — better food, entertainment, décor, prizes, or photography
  • A more impressive event without raising your original budget
  • Access to vetted, reputable local businesses that want to serve your residents
  • A complete sponsor plan shown for approval before anything is confirmed
Part of the fundingCredits your invoice
Part of the fundingUpgrades your event

It's entirely optional: your community decides whether sponsorship is appropriate, which businesses are acceptable, and how visible a sponsor may be. No sponsor is ever confirmed until you approve the business, the offer, the visibility plan, and the event integration.

For local sponsors

Put your business in front of 100 to 1,000 multifamily units at once.

Every Community Spark event is a live, captive audience of residents who live minutes from your door — reach most local businesses can't buy with a boosted post or a coupon mailer. And because your support is what makes the event happen, residents connect your name with something genuinely good happening where they live. It's marketing and community goodwill in the same move.

  • Direct, in-person visibility at every sponsored event
  • Recognition in approved pre-event emails, flyers, signs, or social posts
  • Tasteful on-site branding or a branded event enhancement
  • Opportunities to put samples, prizes, or offers directly in front of a local audience
  • Real, local goodwill — not just an impression you paid for
  • A post-event summary outlining the event and your exposure
Audience reach100–1,000 units
Brand experienceReal, remembered, local

Resident contact information is not automatically shared with sponsors. Any lead collection must be optional, transparent, and approved in advance — your visibility comes from the event itself, not a data handoff.

How the partnership works

Five steps from opt-in to results.

1

Community opts in

The property identifies an event, budget, preferred sponsor categories, and any businesses it will not accept.

2

Partner is matched

Community Spark approaches local businesses that fit the location, audience, event, and community standards.

3

Plan is reviewed

The property receives the sponsor name, contribution, benefits, branding, and resident-facing offer.

4

Event is upgraded

Sponsor funding is divided between a property credit and approved enhancements to the resident experience.

5

Results are shared

Both sides receive a clear post-event summary without compromising resident privacy.
