Privacy Policy

Last updated August 26, 2026. This page explains what information Community Spark collects through this website, how it's used, and who receives it.

What we collect

When you submit a form on this site — a general inquiry, a booking request, or a sponsor sign-up — we collect what you enter, which may include: your name, email address, and phone number; your apartment community's name and website, or your business's name, website, and type; the event address or your business's service area; event details such as date, time, attendance, budget, and requested inclusions; and any sponsor-related preferences, such as tier interest or whether you'd like to attend an event in person. We only collect what you type into these forms — we don't track your browsing or build a profile of you.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, prepare a proposal or quote, coordinate an event or sponsorship, and follow up with you about it. We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing email you didn't ask for.

Who receives it

A few outside services help run this site, and each receives some information as a result:

  • Netlify — hosts this site and processes form submissions. Netlify's servers log standard technical information such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces on this site are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address like any web request.
  • Google Places API — see below.

How we protect it

This site is served entirely over HTTPS, so information you submit is encrypted in transit between your browser and our hosting provider. We don't store form submissions in a separate database of our own — they're read directly from Netlify's form dashboard.

Address autocomplete

The event-location and service-area fields on our booking, contact, and sponsor forms suggest matching addresses as you type. To do this, what you type is sent to Google's Places API as you're typing it — before you submit the form. This only happens in that one field; it's a convenience feature, not something you need to use, and typing a plain address without selecting a suggestion works just as well.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies, run analytics, or use advertising trackers of any kind. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is being tracked.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and booking records for up to 24 months from your submission, then delete them. If you'd like your information removed sooner, see "Your choices" below.

Your choices

You can email Griffin.newton@indycollab.com at any time to request a copy of the information we have on file for you, or to ask that it be deleted. This applies to anyone who has submitted a form on this site, regardless of where you're located.

Children

This site is intended for property managers and business owners, not children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can go to Griffin.newton@indycollab.com or (317) 354-5880.
