Why local businesses sponsor

Local marketing that shows up as a favor.

01

The audience is already local

An apartment community is a few hundred households stacked inside a couple of buildings, usually within a few miles of your door. You're not paying to reach a region and hoping part of it is nearby.

02

You arrive as a neighbor, not an ad

Residents meet your name attached to free food, a prize, or an evening they enjoyed. That's a different first impression than an interruption in a feed.

03

Residents move, and movers buy

Apartment residents turn over. New residents need a dentist, a mechanic, a pizza place, a groomer. Being the name they already recognize is the whole point.

04

You don't have to staff it

Want a table? Take one. Would rather just have your name on the signage and the resident email? That works too.

How it works

From founding list to your first event.

1

You get on the list

Two minutes. Your business, your service area, and the level you're interested in. No payment, no commitment.

2

We find the fit

As apartment communities book events, we look for the sponsor whose category, location, and budget match that event.

3

You see the specifics

The community, the date, the event, what your money funds, and exactly how your name appears — in writing, before anything is owed.

4

You approve — or pass

Passing costs nothing and doesn't take you off the list. Some events won't be right for you, and that's expected.

5

Event happens, you get a recap

A short summary of the event, attendance, and how your business appeared. No resident data, ever.

✦

What you get, and what you won't

Your visibility comes from the event itself: signage, the approved resident email or flyer, and being present if you choose to be. Resident contact information is never handed to a sponsor. If an event includes an optional sign-up, drawing, or offer, it has to be voluntary, clearly labeled, and approved by the apartment community first. Every sponsor is also approved by the property before anything is confirmed — including yours.

FAQ

Straight answers before you sign up.

You don't have events booked yet — what am I actually signing up for?

A spot on the founding sponsor list. We're booking our first communities for 2026. When an event near you is confirmed, you get first look at it. You're not charged and not committed until you approve a specific event.

What does my money actually pay for?

It splits two ways: part credits the apartment community's invoice, and part goes toward upgrading the event itself — better food, entertainment, décor, or prizes. Sponsorship never covers the whole cost of an event.

Do I have to show up?

No. Name-only sponsorship costs the same and includes the same recognition — signage, resident email mention, and a post-event recap.

Do I get resident contact information?

No, never automatically. Any optional resident sign-up must be voluntary, disclosed, and approved by the community first.

What if there's no community near me?

We'll tell you honestly, and you stay on the list at no cost until there is one.

Could a competitor sponsor the same event?

We aim not to place two direct competitors at the same event — tell us your category and service area so we can plan around it.

When do I pay?

After you approve a specific event. Nothing is due to join the list.
